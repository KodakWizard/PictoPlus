# Cover artwork prompts

Mode: built-in image generation, ten individual bitmap assets. The app fits these square originals into a portrait cover without distorting the artwork.

Base prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE original flat square book-cover artwork for a small puzzle-game library. Style: Japanese modernist graphic design, mid-century Japanese editorial and exhibition poster sensibility, refined asymmetry, exceptionally strong composition, simple bold cut-paper geometric forms, subtle restrained screen-print ink and paper grain. It must feel like a beautifully designed Japanese art book, not a generic corporate pattern. Limited colour palette, crisp shapes, no gradients, highly readable at 128 pixels. Reserve the upper 23 percent as quiet warm ivory unprinted paper for the application's book title, except where a different upper background colour is explicitly requested. Main visual arrangement in lower 77 percent, small outer margins. Flat edge-to-edge artwork, not a physical book, no perspective mockup. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, characters or calligraphy. No cranes, Mount Fuji, cherry blossoms or ukiyo-e. Specific design:
